## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-25

Initial release for **World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001)**.

- **Core Match-3 Engine**: 8x8 grid, 6 classic 3D-faceted crystal shapes and colors, click-to-swap and seamless drag-and-release input, cascades with escalating multipliers, Line-Clear (`H`/`V`), Flame Bomb (`3x3`), and Prismatic color-clear specials with special combos and chain reactions, idle auto-hint + on-demand Hint button, automatic no-moves reshuffle, and SavedVariables save/resume.
- **100% Custom Art, Contour-Matched Elemental FX & Quiet Audio**: 49 custom 32-bit TGA textures (Azeroth & Polestar 3D crystal sets, contour-matched outer flames and crackling directional lightning for all 6 gem shapes, combined prismatic fire+lightning super gem, gem-masked specular glints, color-matched crystal shards, 3 unlocked window backgrounds, emblem icon, and laurel badge) plus 16 quiet CC0 Ogg Vorbis audio effects.
- **Polestar Theme Sync & Live Settings**: Live `Polestar.Theme` API v2 reader with automatic repaint on skin/scheme changes, bundled Night-scheme fallback, and a live side-by-side Settings window so background, gem pack, size, and FX changes preview immediately.
- **Flight Path Mode**: First-run opt-in dialog, auto-open/auto-close, combat pause/hide, self-learned route flight durations, honest landing countdown/count-up display, points-per-second (PPS) scoring, route scorebook, beat-the-landing streaks, and opt-in auto-open on death (off by default).
- **Achievements & Unlockable Gem Packs**: 18 data-driven achievements with chat-free gold unlock toasts and unlockable gem packs (all window backgrounds unlocked from the start).
- **Guild & Realm-Wide Leaderboards**: Serverless P2P sync over `JEWELED` prefix (`jeweledsync` stealth channel + `GUILD`/`PARTY`/`RAID` fallbacks), deterministic responder ordering, token-bucket flood control, client-side plausibility validation, 14 classic faction PvP guild rank tiers, and a 5-tab Leaderboard window.
